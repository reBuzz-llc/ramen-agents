# The owner's steers, newest first

A steer is the owner's direction for the family: who to work for, for how long, and what to leave alone. The CEO reads
it and sets its own goal from the newest one; the family follows The CEO.

Your current steer is the `steer` line in common-data/local/family.toml (sh scripts/setup.sh asks for it; the dashboard's
first-run page edits it). That file is not in git, so updating the starter never touches it. Until you set one, the
direction is the market and the buyers in your answers.
