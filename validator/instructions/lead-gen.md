# Lead Gen — output format instructions

The bot's own policy (`policy._check_lead`) enforces LD01–LD04 before it writes, so a flag here means drift, a
hand edit, or a rule that changed after the row was written.

Lead Gen's output is rows on the Leads board: Business (title), Website, Business email, Location,
Industry, Company size, Pain point, How can I help (one of the packages), Evidence (URL), Stage,
Found on, Source pages, Found by. A defect is a message to Lead Gen naming the row and the fields to fix
with its `update_lead` action; Stage stays the owner's to move.

## Lead row

- LD01 — Business is present, and one way to reach it: a Business email or a Whatsapp link (a lead with a WhatsApp number and no email is kept; the team reaches it on WhatsApp). The email is on the business's own domain or printed on one of its pages, never guessed. A free-mail address (gmail, yahoo, outlook ...) is fine when the business shows it as its contact and it reads as the business's ("joespizzanyc@gmail.com", "orders.queens@yahoo.com"); one that reads as a person's own mailbox ("jordan.smith1987@gmail.com") is refused. A Website is expected; a business with NO website is a lead too (it may need one most) -- leave Website empty, fill Location (it is told apart by name and city), and put the public listing page it was found on (Yellow Pages, BBB, a city or chamber directory, its Facebook or Instagram page if it opens without a login) in Evidence and Source pages.
- LD02 — An Evidence URL is present (the Pain point may be left empty: the Lead Enricher fills empty cells), and the evidence is on the business's own site or a job board it uses (Greenhouse, Lever, Workable, Ashby, LinkedIn jobs); for a business with no website, the public listing page it was found on.
- LD03 — How can I help is exactly one of the package names on the owner's Packages page.
- LD04 — One row per business: per website domain, or, for a business with no website, per name and city. A second row for the same business is a duplicate.
- LD09 — The Business email is really published by that business: the Validator fetches the row's own website, evidence and source pages plus the usual contact and franchise paths, and looks for the exact address. An address it cannot find on any page it could read is not one to email. Blank it, or put the contact form's page there instead. Rows Lead Gen listed itself are not re-checked: its policy already read the address off a rendered page of the site. A site that cannot be fetched at all counts as unverifiable, not as fake.
- LD08 — Company size is a size, or blank: locations, employees, or both — "~400 locations", "40 staff", "~11 locations, 120 staff". Not a description of the business: what they do belongs in Industry, what is wrong with their site in Pain point.
- LD11 — Contact person holds the founder's name, the person outreach opens with. Once the website is known the rest can be looked up, so a blank here is work still to do, not a dead end. [warn]
- LD07 — Location, when filled, reads City, State, Country: "Springfield, IL, USA", "Manchester, England, UK". A bare country ("USA", "UK"), a single value, or a street address is not enough; where a country has no states, City, Country will do. Blank is fine and is better than a city nobody read off their pages.
- LD10 — There is an Evidence URL: a page showing the pain point is real. An imported row usually has none, which is worth knowing before anyone writes to them. [warn]
- LD12 — Source pages holds page URLs, one per line, never prose.
- LD13 — The Business email is a desk that buys: not press@, media@, marketing@, careers@, jobs@, legal@ or the like. [warn]
- LD14 — Website is the business's own site, never a directory, association or social page (thebfa.org, franchiseoverview.com, LinkedIn, Yelp...). A row whose site is a directory has a directory's email and evidence too, and is not a lead.
- LD15 — A filled-in Contact person is a person's name (First Last), never a phrase, a company, a role or "N/A": a founder nobody read off a page is an invention. Refused at write time. First names alone are fine when that is how the business's own page prints them ("Sam and Pat").
- LD16 — A report of finished work matches the record: every business a 'done' names has an accepted update_lead (or lead) in Lead Gen's own episode log since the work list went out. A 'done' without a write is an invented output. Write first, watch the verdict, then report.
- LD18 — The Business email is deliverable: a row whose "Email check" (the engine's verification service) starts "NOT deliverable" needs a working address from the business's own site, or the row comes off the board. "Email check" is deliverability; LD09 stays the proof the address is the business's own.
- LD19 — A reminder for the owner, not a bot defect: a row whose Result (set by the team; bots never write it) is still "Emailed" 10 or more days after its Result date has had no reply, meeting or other outcome recorded. Follow up or set the Result. With no Result date, the row's last edit counts, and the flag says so. [warn]
- LD20 — The Whatsapp cell, when filled, is https://wa.me/<digits> for a number an opened page prints: the business's own site or a business listing, as a wa.me link, a number labelled WhatsApp, or a plain phone number (a national number gets [leads] whatsapp_country_code). Never a number from nowhere, from a checker or from a guess. Refused at write time when misshapen; flagged in the sweep when none of the row's pages prints it. A row whose Status is "WhatsApp Verified" or "False Whatsapp Number" was settled by a person in WhatsApp Web and needs no page proof; a False number is cleared by the Validator, ruled out at the gate, and the Lead Enricher is asked for another.
- LD21 — The Pain point cell has three parts, one per line (1 the pain point, 2 a fix they can try themselves, 3 what our team proposes):
  "Pain point: <one gap someone saw, and where>" -- e.g. "On their site: menu is a PDF from 2019, no online ordering."; the family's own gap analysis across the business's public presence (site, Google listing, socials, reviews, delivery apps), never the business's own words and never a guess. Not vague ("could benefit from professional website care", "needs assessment", "no obvious pain points", "N/A"), not our package's or product's name (validator/config.toml own_product_words), nothing the bots cannot see (mobile layout, what a form does on submit), and never calling a tool our offer integrates with a problem (pain_integrated_tools): that is a fit, not a gap.
  "They can try: <a fix they can do themselves, without us>" -- e.g. "put the menu on the site as text and add an order link".
  "We propose: <what our team does, from the row's package>" -- prices only from the Rates or a published package email, otherwise "priced after a short call".
  A rewrite (update_lead with pain_point) that is weak or not in the three parts is refused at write time; a new lead is never refused for it, only flagged here. [warn]
- LD22 — "google map location", when filled, is a Google Maps link to the business (google.com/maps/..., maps.google.com/..., maps.app.goo.gl/...): from the coordinates of the business in a places snapshot, or a place page a search showed. Never a link to anything else. [warn]
- LD23 — "Founder LinkedIn", when filled, is the founder's profile (https://www.linkedin.com/in/<name>) or the business's company page (/company/<name>), and "LinkedIn found via" says where, in this order of trust: 1 Own site (the business's About/Team page links it next to the founder), 2 Business listing (BBB, chamber, directory page naming the founder), 3 Press article (names the founder and the business), 4 Search result (a result naming BOTH the founder and the business; a same-name person elsewhere is the trap). The bots never open LinkedIn itself. A profile only for the Contact person in a business role, kept on the board, never in a draft or a public page. Refused at write time when the link or the step is missing or misshapen.
- LD24 — "Contact role", when filled, is one of: Founder, Owner, CEO / President, Principal / Partner, General Manager, Practice / Office Manager, Operations, Marketing, Franchise Development, Executive Director, Other, and goes with a Contact person. The Contact person is the person to talk to: the highest person the business's own pages name, by type -- owner-run shop: Owner/Founder, then co-owner, then General Manager; practice (medical, dental, therapy, law): practice owner/Principal, then Practice/Office Manager; restaurant: Owner, then General Manager, then operations or marketing manager; agency/consultancy: Founder/Principal, then Managing Director, then marketing/operations lead; franchise brand: VP Operations or Director of Technology at the franchisor, then Franchise Development (the local owner for a single location); property/real estate: Principal or managing broker, then operations; non-profit: Executive Director, then operations/development director. The package nudges it: an operations product -> owner or operations; website/marketing -> owner or marketing; a technical product -> founder or CTO. Refused at write time when not one of the options. [warn when there is no Contact person]
- LD05 — No personal data: Pain point, Location, Industry and Company size carry no phone number and no second email address. [warn]

## Owner notes (not checked automatically)

- Stage is Lead Gen's to set at creation (New); the owner moves it afterwards.

- Deleted cells: a cell the marketing team emptied held a wrong value. The Validator reports it to The CEO, who assigns a family member to find the correct value; Lead Gen fills it only when assigned. (Until this evening a deletion meant "useless, never refill".)


## Model-judged at write time (gate 3: one small-model yes/no per write; the engine asks, fails open)

- MG03 [lead] — For each field listed under "The write" (founded_by, location, size -- only the ones present), does the cited page text state it? A field not listed is allowed to be empty: never answer no because a founder, size or anything else is missing. A value counts as stated when the page gives the same fact in another form: a street address or a list of addresses states the City, State, Country ("123 Main St, Greenville, SC 29601" states "Greenville, SC, USA"); a list of 46 locations states "46 locations"; "founded in 2012 by Jane Ross" states the founder. Answer no only when a listed value is absent from the page, belongs to another business on it, or is a guess.
- MG03 [update_lead] — For each field listed under "The write" (founded_by, location, size -- only the ones present), does the cited page text state it? A field not listed is allowed to be empty: never answer no because a founder, size or anything else is missing. A value counts as stated when the page gives the same fact in another form: a street address or a list of addresses states the City, State, Country ("123 Main St, Greenville, SC 29601" states "Greenville, SC, USA"); a list of 46 locations states "46 locations"; "founded in 2012 by Jane Ross" states the founder. Answer no only when a listed value is absent from the page, belongs to another business on it, or is a guess.
- Empty cells on old rows: on a row made before `lead_message_only_since` (validator/config.toml) an empty cell is never flagged; a wrong value on it still is.
- Email domain: the Business email may be on another domain than the Website when that exact address is printed on a page of the business's own site (LD09 checks it); a free-mail address that reads as a person's own mailbox stays refused (LD01).
