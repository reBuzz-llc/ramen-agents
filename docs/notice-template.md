# ramen-agents -- Notice of Electronic Monitoring (template)

> **Template for the employer to adapt. This is not legal advice.** Have local counsel review it before first use,
> and again whenever the app, the law or your use of the data changes. Replace every item in [brackets].
> Use Section A for staff in New York, Section B for staff in the EU/EEA. Add a section of your own for any other country your staff work in.
> If you have staff in more than one place, give each person the section for where they work.

Placeholders used below:

- [Company name] -- the employer's legal name
- [Contact name or role] and [contact email] -- who answers questions about monitoring
- [Effective date] -- the date this notice takes effect
- [Posting location] -- where the notice is posted (for example: the team page in ramen-agents, and the office notice board)
- [DPO name or role] and [DPO email] -- EU only, if you have or must appoint a Data Protection Officer
- [EU representative] -- EU only, if [Company name] is outside the EU

## Section A -- New York notice (Civil Rights Law section 52-c)

**[Company name] -- Notice of Electronic Monitoring**
Effective [Effective date]

[Company name] monitors some of your work activity electronically. New York law asks us to tell you this in writing
when you are hired, to get your acknowledgment, and to post this notice where you can see it. This notice does all
three.

Your telephone conversations or transmissions, email or transmissions, and internet access or usage on work systems
may be subject to monitoring by lawful means. The rest of this notice explains exactly what ramen-agents does.

### What we collect

1. **Clock-in and clock-out times.** These make your timesheet.
2. **Work outcomes.** Emails you send to leads, replies you receive, and tasks you complete in the team queue.
3. **Active window title.** The name of the window in front on your screen (for example, "Inbox -- Mail").
4. **Screenshots.** One sampled screenshot every 10 minutes. Screenshots are blurred by default.

### What we never collect

- Keystrokes.
- Webcam images or video.
- Microphone audio.
- Your browsing history.

### When we collect it

- Window titles and screenshots are taken **only while you are clocked in**.
- When you clock out, recording stops.
- While recording is on, you see a recording indicator on your screen. It has a stop button.

### How long we keep it

- Screenshots and window titles: **30 days**, then deleted automatically.
- Clock-in/out times and work outcomes: as long as we need them for payroll and records, which is
  [retention period, for example: the period required by law for payroll records].

### Who can see it

- **You** can see all of your own records.
- **Your manager** and [other roles, for example: the account owner] can see your timesheet, your outcomes and your
  screenshots.
- Nobody else at [Company name] can see your screenshots.
- Our software provider stores the data for us and does not use it for anything else.

### What we use it for

- To make your timesheet and pay you correctly.
- To make your weekly statement (hours, rate and any outcome bonus).
- To confirm work was done during paid hours.

### What we do NOT use it for

- We do not use it to read your personal messages or accounts.
- We do not sell it or share it with advertisers.
- We do not use it to train AI systems.
- We do not use it outside work hours, because nothing is recorded then.

### Your choices

- **See your data.** Open ramen-agents to see your own timesheet, outcomes, window titles and screenshots.
- **Delete your screenshots.** You can delete any of your own screenshots.
- **Pause recording.** Clock out, or press the stop button. Nothing is recorded until you clock in again.
- **Ask questions.** Contact [Contact name or role] at [contact email].

## Section B -- EU / EEA notice (GDPR)

**[Company name] -- How we use ramen-agents, and your rights**
Effective [Effective date]

This notice explains what [Company name] records about your work in ramen-agents, why, and what you can do about
it.

### Who is responsible

- **Controller:** [Company name], [address].
- **Contact for privacy questions:** [Contact name or role], [contact email].
- **Data Protection Officer:** [DPO name or role], [DPO email]. [Delete this line if you have no DPO.]
- **EU representative:** [EU representative]. [Delete this line if not needed.]

### What we collect

1. **Clock-in and clock-out times** (your timesheet).
2. **Work outcomes:** emails you send to leads, replies, and tasks you complete in the team queue.
3. **Active window title**, while you are clocked in.
4. **One sampled screenshot every 10 minutes**, while you are clocked in. It is blurred.

We never collect keystrokes, webcam, microphone or browsing history.

### Limits we have set

- **One mode only.** Screenshots are always sampled, blurred and deletable by you. There is no setting for
  continuous, unblurred or hidden capture.
- **Work hours only.** Recording runs only while you are clocked in. Clock out and it stops.
- **Work devices only.** The desktop agent is installed only on devices [Company name] provides for work.
  [If you allow personal devices, remove this line and get counsel's advice first.]
- **Sensitive apps excluded.** No screenshot is taken while a sensitive app is in front. This includes
  [list, for example: personal banking, health, messaging, password managers, and private browser windows].
  A window title from those apps is stored only as "excluded".
- **You can see it happening.** A recording indicator shows while recording is on. It has a stop button.

### Why we do this, and our legal basis

- **Purposes:** to keep an accurate timesheet, to pay you correctly, to make your weekly statement, and to confirm
  work was done during paid hours.
- **Legal basis:** our **legitimate interests** (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)) in accurate time records and fair pay.
  For the timesheet and pay, also **performance of your employment contract** (Article 6(1)(b)) and
  [legal obligation, Article 6(1)(c), where your country requires working-time records].
- **We do not rely on your consent.** At work, consent is rarely free. So we do not ask you to "agree" to
  monitoring as the basis for it.
- We weighed our interests against your privacy. That is why recording is sampled, blurred, limited to work hours
  and work devices, and kept for 30 days only.

### Impact assessment

We carried out a **Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)** under GDPR Article 35 before turning on recording.
It is on file. You can ask for a copy at [contact email]. [If you have a works council or staff representatives,
add: "We consulted [works council / staff representatives] on [date]."]

### How long we keep it

- Screenshots and window titles: **30 days**, then deleted automatically.
- Timesheets, work outcomes and weekly statements: [retention period] for payroll, tax and legal records.

### Who can see it

- You can see all of your own records.
- Your manager and [other roles] can see your timesheet, outcomes and screenshots.
- Our software provider ([processor name]) stores the data for us under a data processing agreement.
  [List other processors, or link to the list.]
- [If data leaves the EU/EEA, say where and the safeguard used, for example: "Data is stored in [country] under the
  EU Standard Contractual Clauses."]

### What we do NOT use it for

- No automated decisions about you. A person always reviews before any decision about pay or your job.
- No sale or advertising use.
- No AI training.

### Your rights

You can:

- **See** your data and get a copy (right of access).
- **Correct** data that is wrong.
- **Delete** your own screenshots at any time in the app. You can ask us to erase other data where the law allows.
- **Object** to processing based on legitimate interests. We will stop unless we have strong legitimate grounds
  that override your interests.
- **Restrict** processing, or get your data in a portable format, where the law allows.
- **Complain** to your data protection authority: [name and website of the supervisory authority].

To use any right, contact [Contact name or role] at [contact email]. We answer within one month.

**This notice is also available at [Posting location].**
