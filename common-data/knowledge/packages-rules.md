# Packages: one offer, one toggle

The Packages page holds ONE toggle per product we sell.

- To change an offer (price, headline, pitch, deliverables): republish the SAME package name. That replaces its
  toggle, at most once a day. A new name for a product already on the page is refused by the Validator (PK14).
- Today's packages, one per product: FILL-ME (list them here as they are published).
- A genuinely new product gets its own new package and toggle.
- Prices: The CEO sets the price, from the Package Designer's research and the owner's rates on the "What we have"
  page; where neither gives a figure, say "priced after a demo" rather than inventing one.
