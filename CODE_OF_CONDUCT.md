# Code of Conduct

ramen-agents is built for people starting out with little. We want everyone who uses it, asks about it or contributes to
it to feel welcome, whatever their background, experience or budget.

## Our standards

Good behaviour includes being kind and patient, especially with newcomers; giving and taking feedback on the work, not
the person; and assuming good intent while saying clearly when something is wrong.

Not acceptable: harassment, insults or personal attacks; discriminatory language or imagery; publishing someone's
private information; sexualised content; and any conduct a reasonable person would find inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions and anywhere you represent the project. Report a problem privately
to the maintainers through GitHub's private reporting, or by email to the address on the project's profile. Reports are
read by the maintainers, kept confidential, and answered. Maintainers may remove content, warn, or ban anyone who breaks
this code.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
